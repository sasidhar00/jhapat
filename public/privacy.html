Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

  • Data Collection: We collect usernames, roles, and encrypted passwords to provide core platform services.
  • Bcrypt Protection: Plain-text passwords are never stored; all passwords are salted and hashed using Bcrypt.
  • Image Storage: Custom profile pictures and deal images are stored as Base64 strings within our database.
  • Geolocation Data: We use browser geolocation to detect your city to display "Nearby" deals.
  • Claim History: We store a log of every "Jhapat" claim for your history and merchant verification.
  • Merchant Stats: We track deal performance to provide merchants with real-time analytics.
  • No Third-Party Selling: Your personal data is never sold to third-party advertisers.
  • Cookies: We use local storage to maintain your session and keep you logged in.
  • Data Retention: Account data is retained as long as the account remains active.
  • Security Measures: We utilize Render for hosting and Turso for distributed database management.
  • External Links: We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external merchant websites.
  • QR Code Privacy: QR codes are generated locally and contain only the unique claim code.
  • Admin Access: Staff can view roles solely for the purpose of account approval and support.
  • Data Deletion: Users may request account deletion by contacting platform support.
  • Policy Updates: Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page immediately.
